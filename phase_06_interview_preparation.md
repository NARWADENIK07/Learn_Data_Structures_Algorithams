# Phase 6: Interview Preparation

This phase turns your technical learning into interview readiness. It includes structured revision, pattern recognition, and company-style problem practice.

- Revision sheets
- DSA cheat sheets

## Preparation Strategy

1. Review fundamentals before every mock round.
2. Practice one pattern at a time.
3. Write solutions with clarity and correctness.
4. Explain time and space complexity out loud.
5. Practice talking through your thinking during coding interviews.

## Common Interview Patterns

- Two pointers
- Sliding window
- Prefix sums
- Hashing
- Greedy choices
- DFS/BFS on graphs
- DP over states
- Backtracking with pruning
- Binary search on answer
