Study Strategy

1. Learn the concept

Start with definitions, intuition, and examples before writing code.

2. Practice by tracing

Step through loops, recursion, and pointer movement to build mental models.

3. Compare complexity

Use Big O to compare implementation choices, not just final correctness.

4. Solve repeatedly

Revisit patterns until they become natural and reusable in interviews.
